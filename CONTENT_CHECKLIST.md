# Content and assets needed before launch

The site intentionally avoids inventing accomplishments or contact details. Zavier should provide or verify the following before deployment.

## Identity and contact

- Preferred professional headline and final homepage introduction
- Real public email address
- LinkedIn profile URL
- Final GitHub profile URL
- Current PDF resume
- Portrait or confirmation that the abstract `ZR` treatment should remain

## About

- Verified degree wording and expected graduation date, if it should be public
- Short personal biography in Zavier’s own voice
- Tutoring subjects, audience, and preferred contact path
- Roles or availability language that should appear on the site

## Each project

- One-sentence verified summary
- The problem or motivation
- Zavier’s exact role and contributions
- Important engineering decisions and tradeoffs
- Verified technology list
- Current status and truthful outcomes
- Repository and live-demo URLs, if public
- Screenshots with permission to publish and useful alt text

The current inSync, Blink Pay, and Agora records are visibly labelled sample content. They must be verified, replaced through `/admin`, or removed before launch.

## Poetry

- Final poem text with intentional line and stanza breaks
- Title, optional excerpt, written date, and themes
- Which poems may be published publicly
- Which single poem, if any, should be featured on the homepage

## Launch decisions

- Final domain
- Production hosting approval
- Production `ADMIN_EMAIL`
- Analytics preference, if any
- Social preview copy approval
- Accessibility review of supplied images and documents
